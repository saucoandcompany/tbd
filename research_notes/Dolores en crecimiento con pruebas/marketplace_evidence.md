# Marketplace evidence: faceless-friendly B2B / professional / engineering digital products (observed 2026-10-04)

**Method and limitation (read first).** All observations below were made on 2026-10-04. The research environment's egress proxy blocked direct page loads of gumroad.com (and all *.gumroad.com subdomains), etsy.com, notion.com, udemy.com, producthunt.com, hightable.io, techno-pm.com, classcentral.com, insightraider.com, skillselion.com, authority.md and agent37.com. Every number in this file was therefore observed in search-engine result snippets of those pages (via WebSearch), not on the rendered page itself. Snippets are indexed copies and can lag the live page by days to months; prices shown in sale/discount form may have reverted. Treat each figure as "as indexed on or before 2026-10-04". Where no volume signal was visible in the snippet, the row says so explicitly rather than estimating. The session's search budget was exhausted before Spanish-language Udemy/Hotmart numbers could be collected (see Gaps).

## Key Question 1: Which business/professional/engineering digital products show the most reviews or visible sales (2025–2026) across Gumroad, Etsy, Notion, Udemy, Amazon, Template.net, Creative Market/Envato and Claude Code skill directories?

### Takeaway
The largest verifiable volume signals in the B2B/engineering niche are on Udemy (ISO 27001 Lead Implementer: 24,650 students / 4,691 ratings; Claude Code Masterclass: 11,163 students / 2,412 ratings) and in a handful of Etsy template shops (618 to 11,578 sales). Gumroad compliance toolkits priced $499–$999 and almost all "Claude Code agent pack" products showed no visible rating counts in indexed snippets; the few with visible numbers are small (12–232 sales, 2–9 ratings).

### Inferences
- In the compliance niche, the deepest verifiable demand pool sits in *courses* (Udemy ISO 27001 LI: 24,650 students), not in template packs; template sellers on Etsy/Gumroad show tens of sales (cyveer 83) and hidden or absent rating counts. Courses are faceless-compatible only if pre-recorded; templates monetise the same pain at lower volume but higher price.
- Etsy's high-volume business shops (3.5k–11.6k sales) are design/marketing/website templates, not regulatory documents; regulatory docs on Etsy are a young (shops "1 year" old) and small sub-market.
- Gumroad's own dataset implies a long-tail market: 44% of products at $0 and a $72/month median, so a "visible volume" bar of even ~50 ratings puts a product well into the top tail.
