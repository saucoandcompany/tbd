## Key Question 0 (framing): Deadline-by-deadline dossier — exact dates, legal source, who must comply

### Takeaway
Within the Oct 2026 – Dec 2028 window, the deadlines with firm dates are: Verifactu (1 Jan 2027 companies / 1 Jul 2027 autónomos), Spanish B2B e-invoicing (1 Oct 2027 for >€8M turnover / 1 Oct 2028 for everyone else), CRA full application (11 Dec 2027; reporting already live since 11 Sep 2026), AI Act high-risk (2 Dec 2027 Annex III / 2 Aug 2028 Annex I after the Digital Omnibus), Battery passport (18 Feb 2027), EUDR (30 Dec 2026 large/medium; 30 Jun 2027 micro/small), PPWR (applying since 12 Aug 2026, labelling 12 Aug 2028), Data Act access-by-design (12 Sep 2026, just passed), CBAM certificate sales (Feb 2027). NIS2 Spain, EAA and ESPR/DPP have no fixed in-window date for SMEs yet.

### Inferences
- Only two in-window deadlines bind essentially the whole Spanish SME/autónomo universe: Verifactu (2027) and B2B e-invoicing (Oct 2028 for most). Everything else is sector- or role-specific (software/hardware makers, brand owners, battery importers, commodity traders, connected-product makers).
- The B2B e-invoicing calendar is the most fragile because it hinges on an Orden ministerial whose publication by 1 Oct 2026 I could not confirm.

## Key Question 1: Which deadline has the largest number of obligated small businesses who must act themselves?

### Takeaway
Verifactu (and, two years later, mandatory B2B e-invoicing) dwarfs everything else: roughly 3 million autónomos plus all companies in Spain must change invoicing software or process, with no in-house compliance team. But the AEAT's free app (launched 12 May 2026, claimed to cover ~80% of autónomos) caps willingness to pay at the low end; the paid opportunity is in the ~20% with higher volume, gestorías, and the 2028 e-invoice interoperability step.
